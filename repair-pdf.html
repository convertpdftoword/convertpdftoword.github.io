Salvage Broken Documents That Refuse to Open

Incomplete downloads, interrupted email transfers, or software crashes frequently leave PDF files with broken cross-reference (xref) trailers. Standard PDF readers reject these files with 'Format Error' or 'File is Damaged' messages.

PDFWordPro analyzes raw byte streams, isolates surviving page objects, and reconstructs a clean trailer and xref table to restore readability.

💡 Security Guarantee: All document streams, vector geometries, and text layers are executed 100% inside your browser memory. We never upload, store, or inspect your documents.

What Can Be Repaired?

  • Broken Xref Offsets: Fixes corrupted byte pointers caused by network transfer glitches.
  • Damaged Trailers: Restores missing catalog roots and info dictionaries.
  • Orphaned Page Streams: Re-links floating text and image content into valid page arrays.
