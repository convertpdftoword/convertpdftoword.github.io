How to Convert PDF to Word on Mobile (Android & iPhone)

Updated October 2026
4 min read
100% Free & Verified

Convert documents on the go using your smartphone browser without installing heavy apps that drain battery and collect personal data.

No App Installation Needed

You do not need to download sketchy third-party apps from Google Play or the App Store to convert PDFs. Most free mobile apps bombard you with full-screen video ads or demand weekly subscriptions.

Using a responsive web tool like PDFWordPro directly in Mobile Safari or Chrome is faster, safer, and 100% free.

Step-by-Step Mobile Instructions

  • iPhone (iOS): Open Safari, go to PDFWordPro, tap 'Choose PDF Document', and pick your file from the Files app or iCloud Drive. Tap Convert and save the resulting .docx to Files.
  • Android: Open Chrome, select your PDF from Google Drive or Downloads, and download the finished Word document directly to your device.
