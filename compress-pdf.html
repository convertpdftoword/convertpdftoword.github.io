Shrink Heavy Documents for Seamless Sharing

Email servers frequently reject attachments exceeding 20MB or 25MB, and portal uploads often enforce strict size ceilings. Compressing your PDF solves these transfer bottlenecks instantly.

Our client-side compression algorithm eliminates duplicated metadata dictionaries, optimizes indirect object streams, and repacks vector tables for maximum compactness.

💡 Security Guarantee: All document streams, vector geometries, and text layers are executed 100% inside your browser memory. We never upload, store, or inspect your documents.

Why Local Browser Compression is Superior

  • No Wait Queues: Compresses files in seconds without waiting for congested remote servers.
  • Preserved Text Clarity: Font glyphs and vector coordinates remain razor-sharp.
  • 100% Data Confidentiality: Sensitive financial audits, contracts, and resumes are never transmitted over the internet.

Tips for Maximum Size Reduction

For documents packed with high-resolution imagery, combining compression with our PDF to JPG tool or page cleanup can shave off up to 80% of file weight.
