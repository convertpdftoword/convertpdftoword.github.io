Prevent Form Tampering and Ensure Consistent Printing

Interactive fillable PDF forms often fail to render correctly in certain mobile viewers, and editable fields can be accidentally altered or tampered with after signing. Flattening converts active form fields into permanent static page graphics.

Our engine merges AcroForm field appearance streams directly into the underlying page content stream, locking checkboxes, text inputs, and dates permanently.

💡 Security Guarantee: All document streams, vector geometries, and text layers are executed 100% inside your browser memory. We never upload, store, or inspect your documents.

Why Flatten Forms Before Submission?

  • Tamper Prevention: Prevents unauthorized alteration of entered values and signatures.
  • Universal Display: Guaranteed to render identically on every device, printer, and archival system.
  • ISO Archival Compliance: Essential for legal affidavits, government paperwork, and loan applications.
