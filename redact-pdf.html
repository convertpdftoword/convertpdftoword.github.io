Permanently Conceal Private Information Before Sharing

Sharing legal exhibits or financial records often requires obscuring Social Security numbers, credit card details, or home addresses. Simple highlight markers can often be removed by recipients—true redaction permanently blacks out the content.

Our engine stamps opaque blackout blocks directly into the document's vector streams, rendering obscured details unreadable across all PDF viewers.

💡 Security Guarantee: All document streams, vector geometries, and text layers are executed 100% inside your browser memory. We never upload, store, or inspect your documents.

Why Proper Redaction Matters

  • Irreversible Concealment: Prevents recipients from copying or viewing underlying confidential text.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Meet privacy mandates when submitting public court documents.
  • Client-Side Safety: Your unredacted file is never uploaded to any remote server.
